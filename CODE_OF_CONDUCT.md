# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

PipelineOS is a professional software project. Everyone who participates in its issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, or other collaboration spaces is expected to:

- Be respectful, constructive, and inclusive.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact directly.
- Focus feedback on the work, evidence, and user impact rather than on people.
- Protect candidate, recruiter, customer, and collaborator privacy.
- Keep security reports, credentials, personal data, and confidential business information out of public discussions.
- Follow the project license and respect the intellectual property of the project and its dependencies.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, or deliberately disruptive conduct.
- Publishing private or identifying information without permission.
- Sharing credentials, service-account keys, access tokens, candidate records, resumes, or other sensitive data.
- Attempting unauthorized access, tenant-boundary bypasses, data exfiltration, or abuse of the MCP/API surfaces.
- Misrepresenting test results, security findings, provenance, or external-source data.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional engineering environment.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, restrict participation, or revoke repository access when this standard is violated. Enforcement decisions will be based on the available evidence and applied proportionately.

For urgent safety or security concerns, do not open a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For other concerns, contact the repository maintainers privately through the repository owner’s GitHub profile.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and to conduct that materially affects project participation, maintainers, contributors, users, or the security and privacy of PipelineOS.
